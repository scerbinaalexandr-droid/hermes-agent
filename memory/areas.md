# Life Areas — 12 Domains

> Структурное разделение жизни CEO на домены. Не projects (те — отдельные с дедлайнами).
> Areas — это **continuous responsibility surfaces**. По блюпринту §04.
> Updated: 2026-05-17.

---

## Domain Structure (шаблон)

```markdown
## <Area Name>

Purpose: <зачем эта область существует>
KPIs: <как измеряем здоровье области>
Recurring Actions: <что делаем регулярно>
Review Frequency: <weekly / monthly / quarterly>
Risks: <ссылки на категории в risks.md>
Reminders: <триггеры для cron / Hermes>
Long-term Goal: <horizon 5-10 лет>
```

---

## 1. CEO / Tandem Group

Purpose: общая управление холдингом, стратегия, культура, кадры
KPIs: revenue growth, EBITDA, ключевые retention metrics, рост бренда
Recurring Actions: weekly leadership sync, monthly board review, quarterly strategy
Review Frequency: weekly
Risks: cashflow, strategic, key people dependency
Reminders: Sun 18:00 weekly CEO review
Long-term Goal: self-running divisions, holding-level focus только на strategy + capital allocation

---

## 2. Finance & Capital

Purpose: cashflow, инвестиции, бюджеты, налоги, distribution
KPIs: monthly cashflow, runway, EBITDA margin, debt ratio
Recurring Actions: monthly cashflow review, quarterly tax planning, annual budget
Review Frequency: monthly
Risks: cashflow, strategic
Reminders: 1-е число каждого месяца — cashflow review
Long-term Goal: разделение personal vs business finance, passive income > 50% lifestyle costs

---

## 3. Health

Purpose: здоровье как foundation для всего остального
KPIs: biological age, sleep quality, weight, blood markers (через HEALTH_VAULT)
Recurring Actions: monthly check-ins, quarterly blood work, annual full checkup
Review Frequency: monthly + weekly micro-check
Risks: health risks (см. risks.md)
Reminders: HEALTH_VAULT для labs / protocols (отдельный Obsidian vault)
Long-term Goal: biological age < chronological age, sustained energy 8-12hr work day без burnout

---

## 4. Супруга

Purpose: качество брака, время вместе, общие цели, support
KPIs: weekly date night, monthly trip / experience, weekly deep conversation
Recurring Actions: daily 30+ min undivided attention, weekly date, monthly trip
Review Frequency: weekly (в evening review секция "Family Status")
Risks: family neglect risks
Reminders: daily Family Touchpoint в briefing
Long-term Goal: стабильная жизнь, общие проекты (family planning — health-данные в отдельном HEALTH_VAULT), recovery вместе

---

## 5. Parents

Purpose: забота о родителях, поддержка здоровья, regular contact
KPIs: weekly call, monthly visit (когда geo позволяет), proactive health monitoring
Recurring Actions: weekly call (звонок воскресенье), monthly visit (или video), quarterly health check
Review Frequency: monthly
Risks: family neglect, health (parents)
Reminders: воскресенье — call reminder
Long-term Goal: parents healthy & supported, no regrets about absent presence

---

## 6. Sport

Purpose: физическая база для energy + longevity
KPIs: 3+ sessions/week, recovery quality, performance markers
Recurring Actions: 3x weekly training (gym / running / sport), weekly recovery
Review Frequency: weekly (в evening review секция "Energy Level")
Risks: burnout risks (sport помогает prevent)
Reminders: ежедневная calendar block
Long-term Goal: training как non-negotiable infrastructure, не optional luxury

---

## 7. Learning

Purpose: непрерывное обучение, deep work на mastery
KPIs: books / месяц, courses completed, applied skills
Recurring Actions: daily reading 30+ min, weekly deep dive (3+ часа на one topic)
Review Frequency: monthly
Risks: overload (learning vs operational)
Reminders: morning briefing — Learning Focus поле (Phase 3+)
Long-term Goal: T-shaped expertise — deep в operations management + lean + furniture industry, broad в business / finance / tech

---

## 8. Travel & Recovery

Purpose: travel как business need + recovery как health need (баланс)
KPIs: recovery days / quarter, travel efficiency (business value / day away from home)
Recurring Actions: weekly plan travel for next week, quarterly recovery trip
Review Frequency: weekly + quarterly
Risks: burnout (если travel без recovery), family neglect (если away too much)
Reminders: weekly travel plan review
Long-term Goal: travel под контролем, recovery — встроенная часть calendar, не afterthought

---

## 9. Brasov Apartment

Purpose: личное жильё в Brasov (renovation in progress)
KPIs: renovation milestones, budget vs plan, completion date
Recurring Actions: weekly project review (с контрактором), monthly site visit
Review Frequency: weekly (пока active)
Risks: project delays, cashflow (renovation budget)
Reminders: weekly check-in with contractor
Long-term Goal: completed apartment as comfortable base в Romania для work weeks
Status: active project (см. projects.md::Brasov Apartment Renovation)

---

## 10. Pharma Project Romania

Purpose: новое направление — производство в Romania (pharma)
KPIs: regulatory milestones, partner agreements, go/no-go gate Q3 2026
Recurring Actions: monthly partner sync, quarterly regulatory review
Review Frequency: monthly (пока в discovery), потом weekly когда active
Risks: regulatory, key people dependency, strategic
Reminders: monthly partner sync
Long-term Goal: operational pharma plant in Romania, generating revenue Q1 2027

---

## 11. Personal Expertise

Purpose: глубокая экспертиза в furniture / lean / management как distinctive asset
KPIs: published content, recognized expertise (speaking / consulting offers), applied innovations
Recurring Actions: monthly thought-piece, quarterly conference attendance, weekly deep work block
Review Frequency: monthly
Risks: overload (если consulting takes time from holding)
Reminders: monthly content review
Long-term Goal: recognized expert in lean furniture manufacturing (industry level)

---

## 12. Knowledge Capitalization

Purpose: трансформация personal experience в publishable / teachable assets
KPIs: book chapters written, course modules built, content published
Recurring Actions: weekly writing 2+ hours, monthly content publish, quarterly course module
Review Frequency: monthly
Risks: overload (vs operational), perfectionism
Reminders: weekly writing block в calendar
Long-term Goal: book + course + content empire built on real CEO experience

---

## Notes

- Areas vs Projects: area = continuous, project = discrete with deadline. Brasov Apartment — *area* AND *project* (project is the renovation, area is the long-term home).
- Не путать с goals.md (long-term outcomes по доменам) и projects.md (active deliverables).
- При weekly review каждая area получает короткий status update (см. SOP/weekly_workflow.md).
